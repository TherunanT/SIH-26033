## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

AgriNexa – Smart Farm-to-Market Network

AgriNexa is a mobile-friendly website that connects farmers directly with household consumers and bulk buyers such as hotels, restaurants, hostels and supermarkets.
Farmers can list their products, quantities and prices, while buyers can place orders directly. The system estimates future demand, selects suitable existing collection centres and combines nearby orders for shared delivery.

Its main features include:

Direct farmer-to-buyer connections.
Demand forecasting to reduce surplus and shortages.
Shared delivery to reduce transportation costs.
Transparent pricing to improve farmer earnings and consumer affordability.
Order tracking for better coordination.

## Technical Approach

Technologies to be used:

HTML, CSS and JavaScript: Website interface.
Python with Flask: Backend and order processing.
SQLite: Storage for users, products and orders.
Python-based forecasting: Uses past orders and averages to estimate demand.
OpenStreetMap: Optional location and route planning support.

Methodology:

Farmers list available products.
Consumers and bulk buyers place orders.
The system estimates demand and checks stock.
Suitable collection centres and shared delivery routes are selected.
Products are delivered, and customers can track their orders.

## Feasibility and Viability

Feasibility: The system uses accessible technologies and existing collection centres, making it suitable for gradual implementation at relatively low initial cost.

Challenges:

Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.
Inaccurate forecasts when historical data is limited.
Transportation costs and spoilage of perishable products.

Solutions:

Provide a simple interface with regional-language support.
Start with basic demand calculations and improve predictions over time.
Combine nearby orders and minimize unnecessary handling and travel.

## Impact and Benefits

Economic: Better earning opportunities for farmers and potentially lower prices for consumers.
Social: Improved market access for small farmers and convenient purchasing for customers.
Environmental: Fewer unnecessary delivery trips and reduced food wastage.
Operational: Better coordination of orders, stock and transportation.

AgriNexa aims to create a more efficient agricultural supply chain where farmers earn better returns and consumers receive affordable produce.
